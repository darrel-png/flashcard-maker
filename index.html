🤖 AI Agent (Free Browser Model)

AI is always on for all users. Uses a free open-source model (GPT-2) that runs directly in your browser — no API key, no sign-up, no cost. The model downloads once (~122 MB) and caches locally. AI-generated flashcards are automatically labeled and synced to your Firebase account when signed in.

Share Questions via Link

Generate a shareable link to send flashcards to another user. They open the link and the app imports the cards automatically.

Anyone with this link can import your flashcards. The link encodes all cards for the selected subject(s) as a compact base64 string.

📊 Share Test History (Improve Questions)

Export your wrong answers and test results to help improve multiple-choice distractor generation. Or import data from other users to enhance your local distractors.

Imported wrong answers are merged with your local history — up to 20 entries per question are kept, improving distractor quality for everyone.
